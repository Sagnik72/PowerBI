# 📊 Tushit Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

Welcome to my **Power BI project** -- an interactive **Ecommerce Sales
Dashboard** that provides deep insights into business performance across
multiple dimensions like sales, profit, customers, payment modes, and
product categories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard has been designed to help stakeholders **analyze
ecommerce sales data** efficiently. It answers key business questions
such as:

-   💰 What is the total revenue, profit, and sales quantity?
-   📍 Which states contribute the most to sales?
-   👥 Who are the top customers?
-   🛒 Which product categories and items drive maximum profit?
-   💳 Which payment methods are most preferred by customers?
-   📆 How do sales and profits vary across months and quarters?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📌 Key Features

✔️ **KPI Cards** -- Highlighting total **Sales Amount, Quantity, Profit,
and Average Order Value (AOV)**.\
✔️ **Sales by State** -- A bar chart showing state-wise contribution to
revenue.\
✔️ **Top Customers** -- Quick glance at best-performing customers.\
✔️ **Category Analysis** -- Donut chart displaying product category
distribution.\
✔️ **Payment Mode Analysis** -- Payment preferences such as COD, UPI,
EMI, etc.\
✔️ **Monthly Profit Trends** -- Bar chart comparing profit
month-over-month.\
✔️ **Product Profitability** -- Bar chart of profit earned across
different products.\
✔️ **Quarterly Filter** -- Allows switching between Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4
views.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

-   **Power BI Desktop** -- Data visualization & dashboard creation
-   **DAX** -- Custom calculations & measures
-   **Data Cleaning** -- Performed within Power Query

## 🔍 Insights Gained

-   Clothing contributes **61% of total quantity sold**, dominating the
    market.\
-   **Cash on Delivery (44%)** is the most used payment method.
-   Top customers include **Farah, Priyanka, Vrinda, and Bhavana**.
-   **April** recorded the highest profit in Q1.
-   Products like **Tables and Printers** are driving significant
    profit.
